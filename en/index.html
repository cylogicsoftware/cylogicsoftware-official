About Us

More than a web agency: a technology partner covering software, automation and digital transformation end to end.

Cy Logic Software designs solutions that bring a company's finance, operations, people and customer processes together on a single digital backbone. Every project starts with understanding how the business actually works, so the systems we build are tailored, scalable and built to last.

By combining modern web technologies, secure infrastructure and AI that can run on-premises, we help businesses keep control of their data and make faster, better-informed decisions.

  • Built around your business

    Software designed for your processes rather than forced into a template.

  • Security by design

    Secure development practices, backups and monitoring as standard.

  • End-to-end delivery

    One partner from analysis and launch to integration and infrastructure.
